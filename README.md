## Objective  

- This task deepens my understanding of how **transistor-level circuit properties** — including **device physics, sizing, and variation** — affect the **timing behavior** that **Static Timing Analysis (STA)** evaluates.  

- By exploring **CMOS design and SPICE simulations** (as done using the **Sky130 process**), I’ve gained insight into the **real transistor-level behavior** behind STA approximations.  

Through this, I developed stronger intuition about:    
- **Threshold voltage behavior**  
- **Noise margins**  
- **Impact of process and supply variations on circuit timing**

## Week Summary
During **Week 4**, I explored the complete **transistor-level behavior** of CMOS circuits using the **Sky130 PDK**.  
Key learnings include:  
- Understanding **NMOS and PMOS device operation** at different bias conditions.  
- Analyzing **velocity saturation** effects and **VTC behavior**.  
- Evaluating **switching threshold, rise/fall delays**, and **noise margins**.  
- Studying **power supply and device variation robustness** to assess design stability.  

This week provided a strong foundation in **CMOS device-level understanding**, linking theoretical principles with **SPICE simulations** for real-world validation.

Week 4 concluded with a comprehensive understanding of how CMOS circuits behave at both the **device and circuit levels**.
